Canadarm 2 Simulation

Simulating a cosmic catch in Gazebo

See Full Report

Background

The Canadarm2 is a 17 meter long robotic arm on board the ISS that performs station maintenace, inventory management, and cosmic catches, in which the arm grapples visiting vehicles and docks them to the ISS.

Project

The goal of the project is to simulate a cosmic catch in gazebo, with an emphasis on smooth and collision free motion planning in order to protect the contents of the payload. This project involved:

  • Creating CAD of spacecraft and the Canadarm
  • Converting the CAD into: URDF, Transmission, Gazebo, Materials, and Meshes files
  • Establshing controllers, configurations (kinematics, joint limits, collisions), motion planning, launch files, and worlds
  • Programming a spacecraft spawn node
  • Creating a motion plan through joint space and operational space commands with velocity and acceleration constraints
  • Integrating all packages into one launch file
