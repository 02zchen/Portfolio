Helios and Hermes

A 6U deployable Cubesat featuring a separation event, variable reefing chute, landing legs,solar panel charging, and grid fins. Part of the 2023-2024 Duke Aero Rocket, Perseus.

See Full Report

Background

Duke Aero is the premier high-powered rocketry team at Duke University that competes at IREC each June. For the 2023-2024 season, Duke Aero designed, fabricated, tested, and launched a Student Research and Developed (SRAD) solid fueled vehicle that flew in the 10K category - Pitchfork. Teams competing within Spaceport America Cup (now IREC) can opt into participating into the SDL Payload Challenge, in which the vehicles fly with an SRAD Cubesat (an industry class of Nanosatellites) that can demonstrate some form of scientific or technical experiment.

Helios and Hermes

Helios and Hermes are indivudally 3U deployable cubesats that combine into a 6U cubesat, refered to as "The Log". Helios, the upper cubesat, showcases a variable reefing parachute system to control descent velocity throughout flight, solar panels, landing legs, and the lumberjack mechanism. When fully integrated, Hermes sits below Helios and is mechanically docked through the lumberjack mechanism. Hermes features gridfins for roll control and a high quality live video feed. The log sits within a passive, mechanical deployer inside the nosecone section of the rocket. Upon reaching apogee, the deployer utilizes shock cords to transfer the force from the primary separation event for the rocket (deployment of drogue) towards for yanking the log outside of the vehicle, after which Helios deploys its main parachute at full aperature. After descending to 2500 ft, the lumberjack mechanism separates Hermes from Helios. This event triggers the deployment of Helios' landing legs, deploys the solar panels, and activates the variable reefing mechanism to gradually reef the parachute into a smaller effective area to match descent speeds with Helios. Simulataneously, Helios deploys it own parachute and activates the grid fins to stablize the live video feed.
